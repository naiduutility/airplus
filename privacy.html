Air+ privacy policy

Effective 30 October 2026 · Air+ home

Air+ is a personal, single-user app. Its only user is its owner, who also runs it.

Data Air+ accesses

With the owner's permission, Air+ reads from the Google Health API: activity and fitness data (calories, workouts, steps, distance, active zone minutes, VO₂ max), health metrics (heart rate, resting heart rate, heart rate variability, blood oxygen, breathing rate, temperature, weight), sleep, and nutrition and hydration logs. It writes only the water and food entries the owner logs in Air+.

How the data is used

Only to calculate the owner's own calorie targets, logs, progress and recovery insights. It is not used for advertising, profiling, or any purpose other than providing these features to the owner.

Where the data is stored

In the owner's own Google account: a Google Sheet in the owner's Google Drive and, later, a database in the owner's own Google Cloud project. Sign-in credentials are held privately by the app's Google Apps Script project. Air+ has no other servers.

Sharing

Air+ does not sell, share or transfer the data to anyone. No people other than the owner can read it.

Google API Services

Air+'s use and transfer of information received from Google APIs adheres to the Google API Services User Data Policy, including the Limited Use requirements, and to the Google Health API Developer User Data Policy.

Removing access and data

Access can be revoked at any time at myaccount.google.com/permissions. Deleting the Air+ Sheet and Google Cloud project deletes all stored data.

Contact

Questions about this policy can be sent to the support email shown on Air+'s Google sign-in screen.
