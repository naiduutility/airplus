Air+

A personal fitness tracker that works with Fitbit Air.

Air+ is a private, single-user tool. It reads the owner's own activity, sleep and health data from the Google Health API and turns it into a realistic daily calorie target, food and water logging, and simple recovery insights.

It is not a public service: only its owner signs in, and the data stays in the owner's own Google account.

Privacy policy

Air+ is an independent personal project and is not affiliated with or endorsed by Google or Fitbit.
